# Vision — AU Smart Lost & Found

## One-line vision
A secure, auditable campus lost-and-found system that returns lost items to their real owners quickly, without exposing private data.

## Problem today
- Lost items are handled manually with no tracking.
- Owners are rarely notified; items pile up at desks.
- No privacy control for photos, IDs, or personal details.
- No accountability for who handled, approved, or returned an item.

## Solution
A digital workflow connecting:
- **Finder** (physically hands item to guard/reception)
- **Guard** (logs item, verifies pickup)
- **Student** (reports loss, claims, picks up)
- **Admin** (reviews risky claims, resolves disputes)

## Principles
1. Privacy-first: photos private, faces blurred, EXIF stripped, sensitive fields encrypted.
2. Chain of custody: items are only "in the system" when a guard/reception physically holds them.
3. Simple for guards: intake under ~30 seconds.
4. Auditable: every important action is logged.
5. Boring and reliable tech: core flow must work even if AI features are off.

## Success metrics
- Item return rate
- Median time from found → returned
- Guard intake time per item
- False-claim / dispute rate
- Student and guard satisfaction

## Non-goals (v1)
- No peer-to-peer handover (students never hold claimed items).
- No public browsing of found items.

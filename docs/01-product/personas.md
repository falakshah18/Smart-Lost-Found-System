# Personas

## 1. Guard (security staff)
- Goal: log items fast, verify pickups confidently.
- Pain: slow tools, unclear ownership, disputes at desk.
- Needs: simple intake screen, QR/code verification, obvious escalate button.

## 2. Student (lost item owner)
- Goal: get my item back with minimum effort.
- Pain: doesn't know where to look; fears privacy leaks.
- Needs: clear status timeline, pickup deadline/location/code, support contact.

## 3. Finder (student/staff/visitor who finds an item)
- Goal: hand it over quickly and be done.
- Pain: doesn't want responsibility for the item.
- Needs: physical drop-off only; no app burden. (System records `intake_source`.)

## 4. Admin (lost & found manager)
- Goal: control risky claims and resolve disputes.
- Pain: no visibility, no audit trail.
- Needs: pending queue, score breakdown, approve/reject with reason, dispute view.

## 5. AU IT / Security reviewer (stakeholder)
- Goal: safe data handling and approved integrations.
- Needs: SSO, private storage, retention policy, auditability, no unapproved third parties.
